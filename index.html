How it works, in 27 seconds

See it for your team

How we work.Master it first. Then make it better.

Shu, ha, ri is a Japanese way of describing mastery: follow the form, break from it, then move beyond it. It’s how we approach every process we touch.

守破離

  1. 01守 Shu

    Learn it as it is

    We run your process the way it runs today, until we know why each step exists and who relies on it. Nothing changes until we understand it.

    In practice

    • A plan that set next year’s targets from this year’s mix. Running it as it stood showed the flaw: whatever slipped was never planned back up, and whatever did well was asked for more than it could give.
    • Time with front-line teams surfaced small rules that cost more goodwill than they saved. Leadership agreed to change them.

  2. 02破 Ha

    Change what holds it back

    Then we test every part: keep it, automate it, simplify it or stop it. Each change is checked against what the customer on the other end needs.

    In practice

    • A weekly task cut from 30 minutes to 3. Monday reporting that took two to three hours to work through became a five-minute read.
    • Analysis that was rebuilt by hand every time now comes from source data structured once and queried again and again: hours back each day, and fewer late nights.

  3. 03離 Ri

    Rethink it entirely

    Once the work is understood, we redesign it around where you’re going, and coach your team on the why as well as the how, until they can run it without us.

    In practice

    • A report that used to describe the week now says what to act on: which orders to speed up and which to cancel.
    • A team member who once needed help whenever a spreadsheet broke now uses AI to find and fix the fault herself, without having to know how every formula works.
